LeonAid combines a mobile acquisition workspace, a back office for charity
administrators and finance roles, and public campaign pages. The visible
product interface currently uses German labels. The English documentation
therefore quotes those labels unchanged.

## Start with your task

- **New to acquisition:** [Capture your first customer order](/en/user/tutorials/first-customer-order/).
- **Prepare a campaign:** [Manage delivery windows](/en/user/how-to/manage-delivery-windows/).
- **Order without an account:** [Place a public order](/en/user/how-to/place-public-order/).
- **Record incoming funds:** [Record an invoice payment](/en/user/how-to/record-invoice-payment/).
- **Cannot sign in:** [Regain access](/en/user/how-to/regain-access/).

Use [roles](/en/user/reference/roles/) and
[statuses and fields](/en/user/reference/statuses-and-fields/) for lookup. The
explanations of [campaigns, contacts, and orders](/en/user/explanation/actions-contacts-orders/)
and [campaign-scoped permissions](/en/user/explanation/action-permissions/)
show why people see different data for the same campaign.
