**Role:** Charity admin of the campaign. Open the campaign in the back office
and choose **Bearbeiten**. All settings apply at once, also while the campaign
runs. The public page shows them from the campaign's data; you do not need to
repeat dates, prices or the order deadline in the campaign page texts.

## Maintain offerings

The **Angebote** section lists each offering with **Name**, **Einheit**,
**Stück**, **Preis**, **Spendenanteil** and **Bestellschluss**.

1. Change the values or choose **Angebot hinzufügen**, for example for a
   second box size.
2. **Spendenanteil** is the part of the unit price that goes to the
   beneficiaries. The public page names it next to the price, for boxes also
   per piece. Leave it empty to state no share.
3. **Bestellschluss** is the moment in your local time after which the order
   form no longer offers the offering. When every offering has one, the
   campaign page shows the latest with a countdown.
4. Switch off **Im Bestellformular anbieten** to hide an offering.
5. Choose **Angebote speichern**. The message **Die Angebote wurden
   gespeichert.** confirms the change.

Existing orders keep the price and size they were placed with. An offering
that orders contain cannot be removed; switch it off instead.

## Order form extras

The **Zusätze im Bestellformular** section has three text fields. Empty
fields do not appear on the form.

- **Newsletter-Einwilligung:** text of a voluntary checkbox, for example for
  news about the club's activities. When a buyer ticks it, LeonAid stores a
  consent with the wording; it can be revoked like any consent through the
  privacy workflows.
- **Foto-Einwilligung:** text of a voluntary checkbox for photos at the
  delivery. The order shows **Fotos: Veröffentlichung von Lieferfotos
  erlaubt**, and drivers see **Fotos für Veröffentlichung erlaubt** on their
  tour.
- **Hinweis zum Widerrufsrecht:** shown above the order button. Have the
  wording checked legally.

Choose **Zusätze speichern**. Each order records the wording its buyer agreed
to; a changed text applies to new orders.
