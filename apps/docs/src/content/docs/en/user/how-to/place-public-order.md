You need the published campaign link and no LeonAid account. Enter real contact
details only on an approved installation; use synthetic data for tests. The
form addresses customers formally ("Sie").

1. Open the campaign page and select **Jetzt bestellen**.
2. Under **Ihre Auswahl** (your selection), set the quantity with **−** and
   **+** or type it, and check **Aktueller Bestellwert** (current total).
3. Under **Wer bestellt?** (who orders), choose **Firma oder Verein** (company
   or association) or **Privatperson** (private person). For a private person
   the company field disappears unless the specific form requires a company.
   Enter first name, family name, and email.
4. Under **Lieferung**, enter recipient, street, postal code, and city. When
   delivery planning is active, also select an available delivery window.
   Windows with the note **ausgebucht** (fully booked) cannot be selected. The
   delivery contact name and phone are independently optional.
5. Keep **Rechnungsadresse entspricht der Lieferadresse** selected or complete
   every separate billing field.
6. If the campaign offers it, you can enter a **Spendenbetrag in €** under
   **Zusätzlich spenden** (donate in addition), from 1.00 € to 10,000.00 €.
   The field is optional. The donation is not part of the order total: it
   stands in a line **Spende** of its own below it. The form names who
   receives the donation.
7. Under **Datenschutz**, acknowledge the data-processing notice and **Ich bestelle die
   gewählte Menge verbindlich.** Submit with the displayed order button.

If something is missing, a list **Bitte prüfen Sie …** (please check) appears
above the form with a link to each affected field. Your entries stay.

The confirmation displays **Bestellung eingegangen**, **Danke für Ihre
Bestellung!** and a reference in the form `LA-…`. Keep it for questions. The
invoice follows separately after the club reviews the order.

If you entered a donation, you get a separate e-mail with the donation pledge
("Spendenzusage"): a payment request with the donation account, separate from
the invoice. The order confirmation names the donation in a paragraph of its
own. After the payment arrived the club issues the donation receipt.

Shortly after, the order confirmation **Ihre Bestellung bei …** (your order
at …) arrives at the e-mail address you entered. It contains the order number,
the items with quantity and price, the total, the chosen delivery window, the
delivery and billing addresses, a link to the campaign page, and the provider,
contact and links to the Impressum and the privacy policy (`/datenschutz`) from
the club's legal settings. It does not repeat the message to the club or the
delivery contact. Each order sends one confirmation, even if you submit the form
more than once. If the order is cancelled before the confirmation goes out,
only the cancellation notice is sent. One address gets at most ten order
e-mails per hour. The form refuses line breaks and invisible control
characters in names and addresses, and web or e-mail addresses in the name,
street and city of an address. If the e-mail is missing, check the spam folder and
contact the club with the reference.

If the selected delivery window becomes fully booked or is retired while you
fill the form, the form does not accept the order, refreshes the windows and
links the choice in the **Bitte prüfen Sie …** list. Select another window and
send again.

If the offering or delivery window changes while you fill the form, LeonAid
retains your fields, shows the current total, and asks for confirmation again.
Review and resubmit. Repeated clicks for the same unchanged transaction do not
create a second order.
