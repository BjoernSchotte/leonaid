You need the published campaign link and no LeonAid account. Enter real contact
details only on an approved installation; use synthetic data for tests. The
form addresses customers formally ("Sie").

1. Open the campaign page and select **Jetzt bestellen**.
2. Under **Ihre Auswahl** (your selection), set the quantity with **−** and
   **+** or type it, and check **Aktueller Bestellwert** (current total).
3. Under **Wer bestellt?** (who orders), choose **Firma oder Verein** (company
   or association) or **Privatperson** (private person). For a private person
   the company field disappears unless the specific form requires a company.
   Enter first name, family name, and email.
4. Under **Lieferung**, enter recipient, street, postal code, and city. When
   delivery planning is active, also select an available delivery window. The
   delivery contact name and phone are independently optional.
5. Keep **Rechnungsadresse entspricht der Lieferadresse** selected or complete
   every separate billing field.
6. Under **Datenschutz**, acknowledge the privacy notice and **Ich bestelle die
   gewählte Menge verbindlich.** Submit with the displayed order button.

If something is missing, a list **Bitte prüfen Sie …** (please check) appears
above the form with a link to each affected field. Your entries stay.

The confirmation displays **Bestellung eingegangen**, **Danke für Ihre
Bestellung!** and a reference in the form `LA-…`. Keep it for questions. The
invoice follows separately after the club reviews the order.

If the offering or delivery window changes while you fill the form, LeonAid
retains your fields, shows the current total, and asks for confirmation again.
Review and resubmit. Repeated clicks for the same unchanged transaction do not
create a second order.
