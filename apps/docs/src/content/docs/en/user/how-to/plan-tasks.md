**Role:** every active account. Open **Aufgaben** (tasks) in the back office
(`/admin/tasks`) or in the app (`/app/tasks`). Both show the same lists and
tasks. Your installation may have switched the module off; then the entry is
missing.

## Views

The views **Mein Plan** (my plan), **Für mich** (mine), **Alle offenen** (all
open), **Nach Liste** (by list) and **Erledigt** (done) sit at the top; in a
list **Offen**, **Für mich**, **Alle** and **Erledigt**. **Filter** (key `F`),
grouping and sorting adjust a view; **Ansicht speichern** keeps it in this
browser.

## Create and share a list

1. Select **Liste wechseln**, then **Neue Liste**, enter **Name der Liste**
   and select **Liste anlegen**. The list is first visible to you only.
2. To share it, open **Zugriff verwalten** in the list. Add an existing active
   account by e-mail address and choose **Lesen** (read) or **Bearbeiten**
   (edit).
3. A list can belong to a charity campaign. Its campaign roles then also grant
   access; removing an extra member does not remove a campaign membership.

## Add and edit tasks

1. Select **Aufgabe anlegen** (key `C`). Type the title into **Aufgabe schnell
   erfassen** and press Enter; outside a list you pick the list next to it.
   The task starts with status **Offen** (open). In **Mein Plan** it lands
   under **Heute** (today) at once.
2. Select a task (or `J`/`K` and Enter) to open its details on the right, as a
   full page in the app. Set a description, a responsible person under
   **Zuständigkeit auswählen**, a section under **Abschnitt auswählen** and
   dates under **Termine und Wiedervorlage**. Select **Speichern**.
3. Under **Schritte** (steps), add steps with **Neuer Schritt** and **Schritt
   hinzufügen**, tick them or remove them. Every change is saved at once.
4. In **Priorität und Schlagworte** (priority and tags), mark **Hohe
   Priorität**, tick tags of the list or create one with **Neues Schlagwort**
   and **Schlagwort anlegen**. Select **Speichern**. The list then shows the
   priority, the tags and the progress of the steps.
5. Status (`S`), plan (`W`), assignee (`A`) and priority (`P`) also change
   right in the list, for one row or all rows checked with `X`. **Erledigt**
   in the selection bar completes the checked tasks. After a change the
   message offers **Rückgängig** (undo).
6. **Reihenfolge ändern** (reorder): in a list sorted by **Reihenfolge** and in
   **Mein Plan**, drag a task by its grip, pick the grip up with the space bar
   and move it with the arrow keys, or use **Nach oben**, **Nach unten**, **In
   Abschnitt** and **Für Tag planen** in the details. While the move saves,
   the keyboard focus stays on the grip or button you used; after a keyboard
   move it stays on the moved task's grip, so you can go on from there.

If someone else changed the task in the meantime, saving is refused and your
input stays in the form. Reload the current state and save again. Leaving the
details with unsaved changes asks first.

## Plan your own day

**Für mich** lists the open tasks assigned to you. Under **Persönliche
Planung** in the details you place a task on a day, under **Irgendwann** (some
day) or leave it **Nicht geplant**. Your planning is private: other members
see neither your plan nor your personal order. **Mein Plan** groups by
**Heute**, **Bis heute fällig** (due today or overdue), **Diese Woche**, **Später**
and **Irgendwann**.

Deferring a task (**Zurückgestellt**) hides it from **Offen** and **Alle
offenen** until the chosen time; the **Wiedervorlage** filter shows it. The due
date stays unchanged.
