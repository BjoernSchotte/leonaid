**Role:** every active account. Open **Aufgaben** (tasks) in the back office
(`/admin/tasks`) or in the app (`/app/tasks`). Both show the same lists and
tasks. Your installation may have switched the module off; then the entry is
missing.

## Create and share a list

1. Select **Neue Liste**, enter **Name der Liste** and select **Liste anlegen**.
   The list is first visible to you only.
2. To share it, open **Zugriff verwalten**. Add an existing active account by
   e-mail address and choose **Lesen** (read) or **Bearbeiten** (edit).
3. A list can belong to a charity campaign. Its campaign roles then also grant
   access; removing an extra member does not remove a campaign membership.

## Add and edit tasks

1. In a list, type into **Aufgabe schnell erfassen** and confirm. The task
   opens with status **Offen** (open).
2. Select the task to open its details. Set a description, a responsible
   person under **Zuständigkeit auswählen**, a section under **Abschnitt
   auswählen** and dates under **Termine und Wiedervorlage**. Select
   **Speichern**.
3. In **Priorität, Tags und Checkliste**, mark **Hohe Priorität**, tick tags of
   the list or create one with **Neuer Tag** and **Tag anlegen**, and add steps
   with **Neuer Schritt** and **Schritt hinzufügen**. Select **Struktur
   speichern**. The task list then shows the priority, the tags and the
   checklist progress.
4. **Aufgabe abschließen** marks the task as done; **Aufgabe wieder öffnen**
   reopens it.
5. **Reihenfolge ändern** reorders tasks by drag and drop or keyboard.

If someone else changed the task in the meantime, saving is refused and your
input stays in the form. Reload the current state and save again.

## Plan your own day

**Für mich** lists the open tasks assigned to you. Under **Meine Planung** you
place a task on a day, under **Irgendwann** (some day) or leave it **Nicht
geplant**. Your planning is private: other members see neither your plan nor
your personal order. **Mein Heute** shows what you planned for today and what
is due today or overdue.

Deferring a task (**Zurückgestellt**) hides it until the chosen time. The due
date stays unchanged.
